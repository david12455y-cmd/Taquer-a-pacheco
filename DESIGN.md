## Brand & Style
This design system captures the vibrant, festive, and deeply rooted culinary soul of a traditional Sinaloan taquería in Angostura. It celebrates street food heritage, communal warmth, and robust flavor. The visual aesthetic blends **Tactile / Folkloric High-Contrast** with approachable modern digital design: appetizing colors, bold ribbon plaques, and playful typography reminiscent of authentic Mexican rotulación and market signage.

The atmosphere evokes hot corn tortillas, grilled carne asada, fresh key limes, and bubbling salsas served on red diner plates. The audience includes local families, late-night diners, and hungry patrons seeking comfort food via digital ordering or on-site menu discovery. The emotional resonance is welcoming, lively, unmistakably Mexican, and hunger-inducing.

## Colors
The palette is built around warm, saturated, and appetizing tones drawn straight from the street taquería environment:

- **Primary (`#D7191C`) — Rojo Asador:** The fiery, passionate red of charred chiles, hot coals, and classic taquería melamine plates. Used for primary calls to action, top navigation bars, key highlights, and promotional tags.
- **Secondary (`#F9C80E`) — Amarillo Maíz:** Bright golden masa yellow inspired by hot handmade tortillas and traditional signage ribbons. Used for section headers, ribbons, badges, and attention-grabbing chips.
- **Tertiary (`#7CB518`) — Verde Lima & Salsa:** Fresh lime green that evokes cilantro, guacamole, and tomatillo salsas. Used for success states, dietary indicators, freshness tags, and secondary action buttons.
- **Neutral / Text (`#3B1F0E`) — Café Carbón:** Rich, deep roasted coffee-brown that softens pure black, grounding body typography and outlines with a warm, charcoal-grilled feeling.
- **Surface Base (`#FFF8E7`) — Crema Tortilla:** Warm, buttery corn-flour tint used as the primary background canvas, ensuring the interface never feels sterile or cold.

## Typography
Typography channels the spirit of authentic market rotulación without sacrificing mobile readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Rubik`):** Rendered in extra-bold and black weights with heavy, friendly geometric contours. Rubik echoes the cheerful, punchy quality of signage lettering while staying razor-sharp across digital displays. Uppercase transforms are used for ribbon banners and menu category plaques.
- **Body & Captions (`Nunito Sans`):** Warm, humanist, slightly rounded sans-serif that complements the friendliness of the headlines while maintaining high legibility for ingredient lists, portions, descriptions, and pricing tables.
- **Labels & Badges (`Rubik`):** Set in medium-to-bold weights with uppercase tracking for badges, callouts, and phone delivery banners.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics tangible, physical print layers, wooden tables, and bright melamine dinnerware rather than sterile tech blurs.

- **Warm Solid Cast Shadows:** Elements like cards, floating action buttons, and order summaries use low-blur, warm offset shadows (`0px 4px 0px #3B1F0E20` and `0px 8px 16px #3B1F0E18`).
- **Tactile Stacking:** Yellow banner plaques sit elevated on top of red header panels with thin `#3B1F0E` micro-borders, establishing strong comic/pop folk depth.
- **Card Surfaces:** Cards reside on stark white `#FFFFFF` or pale toasted cream `#FFFDF7` containers framed by subtle toasted borders (`1.5px solid #F0E2C8`) to set them cleanly apart from the `#FFF8E7` background.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides friendly, plump forms across buttons, cards, and input fields.

- Standard buttons and cards feature `0.5rem` (8px) base corners.
- Featured dishes, promo hero cards, and modal dialogs adopt `1rem` (16px) corners.
- Special pill treatments (`9999px`) are applied strictly to category pills, price tags, and quick-add chips, mimicking salsa bowls and lime wedges.
- Distinctive ribbon containers incorporate clean chevron/flag cutouts on the horizontal edges.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary (Pedir / Ordenar):* Background `#D7191C`, bold white text `#FFFFFF`, rounded corners (8px), paired with a slight bottom press feedback (`transform: translateY(2px)`).
  - *Secondary (Ver Especiales / Acompañamientos):* Background `#F9C80E`, text `#3B1F0E`, bold label.
  - *Tertiary / WhatsApp Delivery:* Background `#7CB518`, crisp white typography, high prominence for direct orders.
- **Ribbon Header Plaques (Listones de Categoría):**
  - High-impact yellow ribbons (`#F9C80E`) with dark coffee border accents (`#3B1F0E`) and centered black uppercase typography, signaling menu sections (e.g., "TACOS DE HARINA", "CHORREADAS", "VAMPIRAS").
- **Product Cards:**
  - High-appeal visual cards with full-width photography framed with slightly rounded upper corners. Includes a floating price tag pill in `#D7191C` with white text, bold title in `#3B1F0E`, concise ingredient list in `#3B1F0E99`, and an immediate "Agregar +" button in `#7CB518`.
- **Chips & Filters:**
  - Rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), default state in `#FFFDF7` with `#3B1F0E30` border; active state filled with `#D7191C` and white text or `#F9C80E` with `#3B1F0E` text.
- **Input Fields & Quantity Steppers:**
  - Cream background `#FFFFFF` with warm `#3B1F0E` border (1.5px). Focus ring utilizes `#F9C80E` glow. Quantity steppers have large, satisfying tap targets with plus/minus icons in bold primary red.
- **Delivery Announcement Strip:**
  - Fixed or top-bar ribbon featuring phone numbers and local delivery status, set in intense `#D7191C` with contrasting `#F9C80E` and `#FFFFFF` typography.
